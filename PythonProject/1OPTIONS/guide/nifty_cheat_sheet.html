Formulas that run the game

  • ATM straddle ≈ 0.8·S·σ·√T → the market's expected absolute move
  • 1σ move = S·σ·√(d/365) · daily ≈ S·σ/√252 ≈ VIX/16 %
  • ΓATM ≈ 0.4/(Sσ√T) · νATM ≈ 0.4·S·√T/100 · Θstraddle ≈ −V/2T
  • P&LΔ-hedged ≈ ½Γ[(ΔS)² − S²σ²Δt] → realised vs implied decides buyer vs seller
  • Prob ITM ≈ |Δ| · Prob touch ≈ 2|Δ|
  • Time value halves when DTE quarters
  • Fair futures F = S·e(r−q)T (≈ 105 pts / 30d at 24.5k)
  • Event move = √(σf²D/365 − σb²(D−1)/365)
  • Kelly f* = W − (1−W)/R → use ¼–½
  • CPR P=(H+L+C)/3, BC=(H+L)/2, TC=2P−BC

OI, PCR, chain

OI ↑OI ↓
Price ↑Long build-upShort covering
Price ↓Short build-upLong unwinding
  • Max call OI = resistance · max put OI = support · OI change > OI level
  • Put writing shifting up = bullish; call writing shifting down = bearish; call OI unwinding at the wall = breakout tell
  • PCR(OI) rising = support building; >1.4–1.5 crowded long; <0.7 oversold → covering risk
  • Max pain is weak; trade OI concentration and gamma pins
  • FII fut long ratio <20% = short-covering fuel; >70% = complacent
  • Gamma flip: above → fade extremes; below → follow breaks

Order flow

  • CVD on near-month futures (the index has no volume). Price HH + CVD LH = absorption → fade at a level
  • Large delta, no progress = absorber; trade with them once price leaves
  • Stacked imbalances (3+ levels ≥3:1) = initiative
  • VWAP: above + rising = long only; flat = fade ±2σ; anchored VWAP from swings/events
  • Profile: POC fair value; VA 70%; LVN = fast travel; 80% rule on re-entry to the prior VA
  • Book: the level is noise, the 2-minute shift (z) is signal; pulled size = spoof, refilling = iceberg
  • Futures premium shrinking in a rally = weak/spot-led

Playbooks (one line each; backtest first)

  1. Expiry harvest: 09:30–10:00 iron fly on the heavy-OI ATM; wings ≈ 1.2× straddle; SL +30% or spot beyond BE; flat by 15:15
  2. Expiry gamma break: after 13:30 compression near a big strike → OTM long in the break direction (CVD + book); ≤0.3% risk; out by 15:10
  3. ORB + flow: 15-minute range break + 1.5× volume + CVD new extreme + VWAP side → 0.65Δ ITM; stop at range mid; ½ at 1R
  4. VWAP fade: balance day only; 2σ touch + CVD divergence → credit spread; target VWAP
  5. Monthly IC: 30–45 DTE, 0.15–0.2Δ shorts, credit ≥ ⅓ width; TP 50%; roll at 0.30Δ; exit 21 DTE
  6. Event: implied move < history → buy the IV ramp, exit before; > history → sell the fly into it
  7. Gap: inside prior range → fade on failure; outside + holds 30 min → buy the VWAP pullback; >1.2% → no selling before 10:00

Risk rules

  • Risk 0.5–1% per trade; lots = capital·risk% / (stop pts × lot)
  • Sellers: stress (±3% weekly / ±6% monthly, IV +8) ≤ 3–5% of capital; margin ≤ 50%
  • Overnight: a 3% gap must not cost more than 3% of capital
  • Daily −2% stop · weekly −5% → half size · monthly −10% → paper
  • 2 full stop-outs in a row → done for the day
  • Longest losing streak ≈ ln N / ln(1/(1−W)) (50% win, 250 trades ≈ 8)
  • Nifty + Bank Nifty = one bet (ρ ≈ 0.85)

Management and adjustments

  • Sellers: spot-based stops; buyers: premium/time stops
  • Tested strangle: roll the untested side in → convert to IC/fly → close. Max 2 adjustments
  • Credit trades: take 50–70%; the remainder is gamma risk
  • Long options: never average down; exit if no move by ⅓ of the planned time
  • Buy wings first; close the weekly leg of a calendar before expiry day
  • Roll only if you would open the new trade fresh

Pre-market checklist

  1. GIFT Nifty vs fair premium → gap estimate
  2. US close/futures, Asia, crude, USDINR, US10Y
  3. Participant OI (FII long ratio), FII/DII cash
  4. Chain: OI walls and shifts, PCR trend, IV percentile, term structure, skew
  5. Levels: PDH/PDL/PDC, VA/POC, weekly H/L, CPR width, round numbers
  6. Events today (RBI, US data, heavyweight results)
  7. Plan A/B/C for gap up/flat/down; today's max loss

Do NOT trade when…

  • You can't name the setup, the stop level and the size before entry
  • Daily loss limit hit, or after 2 straight full losses
  • Naked short into a binary event (results, Budget, elections)
  • Selling premium on a confirmed trend day
  • Buying options when IV percentile > 80 without a catalyst
  • First 5 minutes with market orders / illiquid strikes
  • Spread > 1% of premium · VIX spiking > 10% intraday with open shorts unhedged

Edge insights (from the manual)

  • Expiry settles on a 30-minute average → late moves pay less
  • Weekend theta is priced on Friday
  • Puts beat calls as directional longs (IV tailwind)
  • After STT 2026, ITM options are a cheaper delta than futures intraday
  • OTM strangles earn early, ATM straddles earn late
  • Three stocks (HDFCBANK, ICICIBANK, RELIANCE) ≈ 28% of Nifty
  • Skew widening with flat spot precedes downside
  • Pre-Sep-2025 backtests come from a different market (Thursday expiry, other lots)
